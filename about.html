Our Story

Crafted by Hand, Inspired by Nature.

Boone & Grain is the creative studio of Adelaide artisan Harriet Boone, who transforms carefully selected timber into distinctive jewellery and wearable accessories.

Meet Harriet

Harriet began woodworking as a hobby, creating small artistic pieces in her home workshop. Her interest gradually developed into a passion for producing wearable items that combine craftsmanship, individuality and the natural beauty of timber.

After moving to Adelaide, Harriet created Boone & Grain to share her work with a wider audience and build a sustainable creative business.

The Workshop

Each Boone & Grain piece is designed and produced in Harriet's small Adelaide workshop. Timber is selected, shaped, sanded and finished by hand, allowing every item to retain its own natural grain and character.

Our Approach

Small-Batch Craftsmanship

Pieces are produced in small quantities, allowing greater attention to detail and reducing unnecessary production.

Natural Materials

Timber is chosen for its texture, colour and grain, making natural variation an important part of each finished design.

Creative Learning

Harriet shares progress videos, tutorials and workshop insights to encourage other makers and document the creative process.
